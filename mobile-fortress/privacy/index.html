Data stored only on your phone: game progress, settings, resumable sessions, reward records, age-range choices and permission/denial records can be removed through Android Settings → Apps → Mobile Fortress → Storage → Clear data (labels may vary). This can permanently remove your progress. We cannot remotely erase these local records. Delete exported save-code copies separately. The app does not provide a developer user account to delete.

Google-controlled data: this email request does not automatically delete advertising SDK data controlled by Google. See Google’s privacy policy and data controls for its deletion options. We can explain the distinction and assist with directing your request, but cannot guarantee deletion by Google. Withdrawing ad permission stops subsequent app ad requests as described below; it is not deletion of previously processed data.

Scope and contact

This notice describes personal-data handling in the Mobile Fortress Android closed-test build 0.3.7. The operator is Tow Jia Fu, operating as Starhaven Games. Contact: albertbuilds123@gmail.com. The operator handles this mailbox personally.

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over, with Malaysia and Singapore as the planned launch markets. This statement is not an official content rating. The current build uses Google test ads only; real commercial ads have not been enabled.

Game data on your device

Progress, settings, resumable game state and reward records are stored on your device to support gameplay and remember your choices. The current game has no developer-provided user account, cloud-save service, or separate developer analytics or crash-reporting service. The Google ads SDK may still process diagnostics.

You can copy a save code and import a code. A save code encodes game records; it is not an encrypted backup. Do not share it publicly. We do not keep a copy for you. Clearing the app's local data through Android settings may permanently remove progress. Copies you exported or shared must be deleted separately. System backup is disabled and data-transfer exclusions are configured in this test build; do not rely on system backup to restore progress.

Age range and advertising choices

The app stores your selected age range (under 13, 13–17, adult, or not specified), permission status, notice version, revision and update time, and a grant or withdrawal time, locally. Updates replace the previous record rather than retaining a complete history. The permission record does not contain a name, email address or exact date of birth and is not uploaded to a developer server.

The 13–17 flow asks a parent or guardian to read the notice and make the permission choice. It does not verify their identity. The under-13 and unspecified-age choices do not allow the app to request ads. You can continue normal gameplay without authorising ads, but will not receive optional rewards that require watching an ad.

Ads are offered on a voluntary basis, not played automatically. Advertising-request settings do not establish that no SDK activity occurs before a permission choice; see the Google SDK section below.

Withdrawal and local storage limitations

You can withdraw advertising permission in settings. The app attempts to save both the permission state and a separate local denial record. If either write succeeds, the app keeps subsequent ad requests disabled after restart. Normal permission choices are remembered between sessions.

If both writes fail, the app disables subsequent ad requests for the current session and displays a warning that the choice was not saved. An older permission may return after restart. If you see this warning, retry saving your withdrawal and avoid using ad features until the problem is resolved; contact support if needed. The app does not automatically erase your game data.

A saved withdrawal does not close an ad already on screen or delete data Google has previously processed. Local permission and denial records are removed when the app's local data is cleared.

Google test ads and SDK processing

The build integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0 with Google's official test identifiers. Preparing an AdMob app or ad unit does not mean real ads are active in this build. Before enabling real ads or materially changing data handling, we will update this notice and provide additional notice or choices where required.

Google's SDK disclosure describes processing of IP addresses, app or advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account-related identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. An IP address may be used to infer approximate location. The actual data processed depends on device, configuration and service behaviour; this notice does not mean every listed category is collected on every device.

Permitted ad requests use child-directed and under-age-of-consent flags, a G content-rating limit and a non-personalised-ad parameter. Android advertising-ID and related AdServices permissions are removed. These settings do not mean the SDK processes no data, and test advertising is not completely offline.

The app includes an SDK automatic-initialisation component. Complete network behaviour has not been independently verified, so we do not promise that no SDK initialisation or data processing occurs before age selection, permission or an ad request. The in-game permission control governs subsequent app ad requests; it is not a deletion control for all third-party processing.
