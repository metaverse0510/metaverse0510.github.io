Word Tower Privacy Policy

English · Version 20 internal test and version 21 candidate; earlier versions 16, 18 and 19 and version 17 candidate

1Who operates Word Tower

Word Tower is operated by Jia Fu under the Starhaven Games brand in Malaysia. In this policy, “I” refers to Jia Fu. I handle privacy and support enquiries personally by email, telephone and SMS. Contact details are listed in section 12.

This policy covers the Android app, this privacy website and support enquiries. It covers the current version 20 internal test (1.0.0-prep.7-age-notice), the version 21 candidate (1.0.0-prep.8-protected-speed), the earlier versions 16, 18 and 19 internal tests, and the unpublished version 17 candidate (1.0.0-prep.4-demo). These versions use the package name com.albertgames.wordtower. Version 17 has been built locally but remains unsigned, uninstalled and unpublished. Its descriptions below concern implemented test controls, not verified behavior with live Google services.

Version 16 uses only Google’s demo rewarded-ad unit. Revival, completion rewards and speed boosts have been exercised on a test device. These tests do not establish that every privacy flow or region is supported. The earlier separate 0.3-adult-demo-test app has also been tested; it is not the current Google Play internal-test package.

The app includes Google Mobile Ads 25.5.0 and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0. Production advertising remains disabled in versions 15, 16, 18, 19 and 20 and the version 17 and 21 candidates. Version 15 also blocks UMP consent-service requests. Versions 16 and 17 have different, restricted adult-test privacy paths, described in section 5. Publishing this policy or a Google privacy message does not turn on production ads or release version 17.

Version 18 has been signed, distributed through restricted Google Play internal testing, installed and tested on the operator’s device. It uses only Google’s demo rewarded-ad unit. It inherits the version 17 adult privacy checks and local privacy-safety records described below, with the additional adult-only configuration test described in section 5. References below to version 17 privacy controls also describe the controls inherited by version 18 unless a difference is stated. The selected device tests do not establish that every privacy flow or region is supported, or that advertising to real child players is compliant. Production advertising remains disabled.

Version 19 (1.0.0-prep.6-protected-demo) was distributed through restricted Google Play internal testing and has been replaced by version 20 on that track. It retains version 18’s SDKs, Google demo rewarded-ad unit and local privacy records, with production advertising disabled. The existing privacy and retention descriptions also apply to version 19, except for the additional notice and protective checks described in section 5. Local build and logic checks do not establish device behavior or legal compliance.

Version 20 (1.0.0-prep.7-age-notice) has been signed, distributed through restricted Google Play internal testing and installed on the operator’s device. The operator confirmed the first-notice button change, the retained “Prefer not to say” option, and opening and returning from this policy without an age being selected automatically. These targeted interface checks do not establish that advertising, rewards or every SDK and regional flow work correctly. Version 20 otherwise retains version 19’s privacy controls and data handling.

Version 21 (1.0.0-prep.8-protected-speed) is an unsigned, locally built candidate prepared only for the operator’s restricted internal test. At this revision it has not been installed, uploaded, distributed or tested with live Google SDK services. It keeps the existing SDKs, adult privacy controls and retention arrangements, but adds the separate protected-age double-speed demo path described in section 5. It remains configured for Google’s demo rewarded-ad unit only, with production advertising disabled. Local checks do not establish legal compliance, suitability for real child users or a five-second ad-exit guarantee.

2Information stored on your device

Word Tower stores game progress, learning records and settings locally to run the game, choose practice activities, adjust learning levels and remember your choices.

  • Game progress, such as currency, experience, wins, unlocked maps, rewards, cosmetic selections, saved battle state and trial records.
  • Learning records, such as questions encountered, correct and incorrect outcomes, hints, word and topic practice, learning levels, and recent learning history.
  • Learning settings, such as a selected learning age range, practice mode, learning target, session duration and break reminders.
  • Preferences, such as language, sound, speech, text size and whether advertising offers are shown.
  • A temporary recovery record if a confirmed local-data reset is interrupted.

The app does not provide a Word Tower account or developer-operated cloud save. Its game code does not upload saved games or local learning records to a developer server. You do not need to give your name, email address or exact birth date to play. Local learning records can still be personal information, especially when they relate to a child.

The adult-test implementations also keep local privacy-safety records, separate from game progress. Version 17 adds privacy-check state and a digest of SDK privacy records for comparison between checks. Their purpose, limitations and retention are described in section 7.

3Age choices and children

Word Tower’s intended Google Play audience is the selected 9–12, 13–15, 16–17 and 18-and-over age groups. Availability is also subject to the applicable content rating in each country or region. The learning age range helps select a starting learning level and is saved with local learning settings.

A separate screen asks for the current player’s advertising age range: under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, or prefer not to say. This choice lasts for the current app session rather than becoming a permanent player profile. The app does not ask for an exact birth date.

Production advertising remains disabled in versions 15, 16, 18, 19 and 20 and the version 17 and 21 candidates. In the distributed versions 16, 18, 19 and 20 and the version 17 candidate, new app-initiated UMP and advertising requests are restricted to players who select age 18 or older. Version 21 adds a separate, limited Google demo-ad path for explicit selections of under 13, 13–17 or “Prefer not to say,” described in section 5. It requires a voluntary double-speed request and a separate one-use confirmation before it may initialize the advertising SDK or load that demo. Selecting an age alone does not start this protected-age request path. This candidate is prepared only for the operator’s restricted internal test; it does not open production advertising to children or establish applicable consent or child-advertising compliance. The limits on already-started SDK activity are explained in section 5.

A parent or guardian can manage learning settings and clear local progress on the device. A settings gate or age choice is not identity verification or verified parental consent to collect personal information.

For concerns about a child’s information, contact albertbuilds123@gmail.com. Do not send a child’s full name, birth date, identity documents or unnecessary personal details.

4Speech and device services

The Android app passes learning text to an installed text-to-speech service and selects an English voice reported by that service as not requiring a network connection. If a suitable installed voice is unavailable, the app does not play the spoken text or substitute a cloud voice.

Word Tower does not record your voice or request microphone, camera, contacts or precise-location permissions. It reads Android’s connection status and pauses play when a validated internet connection is unavailable. This status check does not upload game progress or send the app’s own test request to a third-party server. Locally saved progress does not mean this version supports fully offline play.

Android, Google Play, device-manufacturer services and installed speech services may separately process information under their own settings and policies. The game’s reset does not control those services.

5Test advertising and privacy

Available internal test and unpublished candidate

Version 20 is available through restricted Google Play internal testing and is configured to use Google’s demo rewarded-ad unit, not Word Tower’s production ad unit. The earlier versions 16, 18 and 19 internal tests used the same demo unit. Version 17 is a local, unpublished candidate using the same demo unit. Version 17 has not been installed or tested with live Google UMP or Mobile Ads services. Its local and simulated tests do not establish device behavior, complete regional support or legal compliance. These versions do not enable advertising to real child players or production ad revenue.

The integration uses Google AdMob through Google Mobile Ads and Google UMP. Privacy-message requests can contact Google separately from loading an advertisement. UMP handles privacy choices and their status, including locally stored records used to determine which messages and requests are available.

In the adult-test paths, ad offers are enabled by default unless previously hidden. Selecting age 18 or older while the app is in the foreground can contact Google through UMP before a reward-button tap. The version 17 candidate and version 18 also allow this check before their separate local demo-ad enablement. Showing ad offers again or returning to the foreground may trigger a fresh privacy check. Advertising-SDK initialization and ad loading require an explicit reward request after the eligibility checks pass. The app does not automatically preload rewarded ads. A privacy-service request is separate from an ad request.

Versions 19 and 20: notice and protective checks

Before the neutral age-choice screen, version 19 adds a notice explaining possible contact with Google’s privacy service and a link to this policy. The notice and age selection do not give or change Google privacy consent. Dismissing them does not select an adult age. After an explicit selection of age 18 or older with ad offers enabled, UMP may still contact Google before a reward-button tap; the existing privacy checks and separate local demo enablement remain in place.

Versions 19 and 20 separately check the current player’s age selection, supported privacy conditions and the ad source and format. The app uses the conservative child-directed SDK configuration (CHILD) for current-player selections of under 13 or 13–17, and when the age is unknown. In versions 19 and 20, new app-initiated UMP and advertising requests remain blocked for these age paths. The version 21 exception is described next. This configuration is a safeguard, not proof of a player’s age or parental consent. The adult-only age-treatment simulation described below remains available subject to its existing checks. Version 19 adds no SDK, network provider, data-use purpose or persistent privacy field. These changes do not enable production or real-child advertising, verify a child-suitable ad source, or establish a five-second ad-exit guarantee.

Version 21 candidate: a separate protected-age speed test

This path is implemented for explicit current-player selections of “Under 13,” “13–17” or “Prefer not to say.” It is separate from the adult age-treatment simulation. Selecting an age alone does not initialize the advertising SDK or load an ad through this path. With ad offers enabled and the local checks satisfied, each voluntary double-speed request opens a native explanation. Only choosing “Watch test ad” there allows that single speed-test request. Cancelling the age choice or leaving age unselected gives no permission. Revival and completion-bonus ads remain blocked for these protected-age selections.

For this path, the app sets Google Mobile Ads to the child-directed CHILD treatment and G content rating before initialization and loading, uses a non-personalized request setting and allows only the fixed Google demo rewarded-ad unit. It does not start a new UMP request or form and does not borrow an adult privacy receipt or consent as permission. The Google advertising SDK can still process device and network information, IP-derived general location, interactions and diagnostic information as described below. A demo unit and CHILD, G or non-personalized settings do not mean that no data are processed.

The checks read existing local privacy signals. Only an absence of IAB signals or a narrowly checked explicit non-applicability signal with the required metadata can pass this local test gate. This is not proof of regional eligibility or Google or parental consent. TCF signals, even positive ones, US GPP or USPrivacy signals, unsupported or damaged information, read failures and an observed current adult refusal block the protected path. Once observed, such a blocking condition remains locked for that instance. Earlier history or unfinished-process flags alone are not treated as either refusal or consent.

The one-use permission and its checking state exist only in memory; no new app-managed persistent privacy fields are added. Relevant age, offer or privacy changes invalidate pending permission. Leaving the foreground invalidates pending permission for ads not yet displayed; an already displayed SDK ad is not forcibly closed. These controls do not retract requests already sent, erase provider-held data or guarantee that SDK background work stops. The existing storage, reset and deletion limits in section 7 continue to apply.

Information involved in testing

Google Mobile Ads can process IP addresses and general location derived from them, app or ad interactions, diagnostic information, and available device or account-related identifiers. Google describes advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as purposes for this processing. The information involved depends on the service, settings and permitted identifiers.

Versions 16, 17, 18, 19, 20 and 21 remove Android advertising-ID permissions and use non-personalized ad-request settings. These measures do not remove all SDK information or make requests anonymous. Non-personalized or limited advertising can still involve data processing and device storage; applicable privacy requirements still matter during testing.

Google’s demo ad units show Google ads. A demo unit does not remove Google’s SDK data processing. Regional privacy-message partner selections can cover a broader set of providers and are not limited to Google alone. They can change, and inclusion in a selection does not mean that a partner receives every person’s information. The actual test service and its partner disclosure must match before a build is distributed.

Regional messages and test limits

The configured message for the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland offers consent, refusal and management of options in English and Simplified Chinese. The configured English message for US states supported by Google’s US-state messaging service offers a choice about allowing or declining the sale or sharing of data. This does not mean that the same message applies in every US state or that every regional right is covered by that choice. A configured or published message is not evidence that the app has displayed it. The applicable screen and its partner and purpose information explain the choices being requested.

Version 16 follows a conservative test rule: new advertising requests require a successful, current UMP check, both consent and privacy options reported as “not required,” the SDK permitting requests, and no blocking privacy history, unfinished process, recorded privacy choice or unsupported regional signal. Sessions involving a regional privacy choice are excluded even if the player accepted it.

The version 17 candidate instead checks two limited situations: a reviewed subset of complete Google TCF privacy signals, or an explicit, supported indication that the relevant consent requirement does not apply. It does not infer permission from an absence of signals, from closing a form, or from an SDK status alone. Its TCF checks use fixed, reviewed CMP, vendor-list and policy versions. A later format or version may keep ads off until reviewed. US GPP, older USPrivacy and other unsupported regional signals remain outside this candidate’s supported ad paths. New ad requests stay off if the permissions required by this test are missing, refused or withdrawn, or if the privacy signals are damaged, inconsistent or outside its supported subset. The test does not require consent to every listed provider, and passing it does not establish permission for every provider or purpose. A later non-applicability signal does not erase uncertain older history or an observed conflict.

These limited checks do not establish that consent is unnecessary under every applicable law or that these versions support every region. The version 17 candidate’s behavior with real SDK responses, actual devices and Android storage or process failures has not yet been verified.

Changing choices and local demo-ad enablement

The app’s entry point is “Privacy options” and its menu is “Ad privacy options.” When UMP requires a privacy-options entry, “Advertising privacy choices” is implemented to open the relevant SDK form. Its live behavior still needs verification in version 17. The version 17 candidate closes the gate for new ad requests before opening that form. Closing or cancelling it does not by itself re-enable demo ads.

In the adult-test path, after the supported privacy checks pass, “Enable demo ads with current choices” offers a separate local confirmation. It only enables optional demo rewards in this app. It does not create, replace or change Google privacy consent or supply a missing purpose permission. Each time Android starts a new app process, fresh checks and explicit local enablement are required. A saved record alone cannot enable ads. Within the same already-enabled process, returning to the foreground may restore availability after a successful fresh check of unchanged privacy signals and valid local records, without asking for Google consent again.

“Recheck privacy” in version 17 retries the current checks without resetting UMP or deleting earlier Google or IAB choices. A failed or timed-out check keeps new ad requests off. The app waits for an earlier SDK operation to finish before starting another; a timeout does not mean that operation was cancelled.

“Hide ad offers” disables new rewarded-ad requests and the app’s automatic UMP updates. In the adult path, if you separately choose “Advertising privacy choices,” the app may still contact Google to display or manage that form while offers are hidden. It does not withdraw choices stored by UMP, erase information already processed by Google, cancel requests already sent, or guarantee that background work by an already-initialized SDK stops. Resetting game progress also preserves SDK privacy records and the app’s privacy-safety records described in section 7. Provider-held information follows the provider’s retention and deletion practices, rather than the support timetable in section 6.

Adult-only age-treatment simulation in version 18

An adult tester who passes the existing privacy checks and explicitly enables demo rewards for the current process can separately confirm a simulated Google Mobile Ads age-treatment setting: CHILD, TEEN, UNSPECIFIED or unknown-as-CHILD. This does not change the tester’s real-player age choice, UMP age treatment or Google privacy choices. In versions 18–20, players who select under 13, 13–17 or “prefer not to say” for their real-player age remain blocked from starting new UMP or advertising requests through this app. The separate protected-age speed test in version 21 is not this adult simulation. This age selection is not identity or age verification.

Selecting a simulation updates Google Mobile Ads’ in-process request configuration but does not itself call SDK initialization or ad loading. A later demo reward still requires the privacy checks, local enablement and a voluntary request. The SDK uses the selected age treatment for ad requests; it is not guaranteed to remain only on the device. An already-started SDK may continue background work.

The app keeps the simulation choice only in memory and adds no new app-managed persistent privacy records. Exiting simulation clears the simulated setting; any later app-initiated ad request uses the real-player treatment, subject to the existing eligibility checks. Changes to the real-player age choice or privacy availability, hiding offers and resetting the game can clear the simulation; a new Activity or app process does not restore it. These changes do not cancel SDK work already sent, delete information already processed by Google or forcibly close an ad already displayed. This adult-controlled configuration test does not verify parental consent, real-child advertising, permitted ad sources or a five-second exit requirement.

6Support enquiries and retention

This section describes how I handle support enquiries under this policy.

I accept enquiries by email, telephone and SMS. I do not offer support by post. The address in section 12 is provided for operator identification, not as a postal customer-support service. This does not limit rights or obligations under applicable law.

If you contact me by telephone, I receive the information you tell me and may see your telephone number. If you send an SMS, I receive its content and available sender information. Telephone and SMS providers may process communications and delivery information under their own policies.

For telephone support, I will not actively record calls and will keep only notes needed to resolve the enquiry. I will delete customer-support SMS messages and related records I keep within one week after the issue is resolved. If a child provides personal information by telephone or SMS, I will delete that information from the messages and related records I keep as soon as possible and ask a parent or guardian to contact me separately.

These deletion arrangements apply to the support records I control. Deleting my copies does not erase records retained separately by telephone or SMS providers, or copies held by the sender or other recipients. Providers apply their own retention and deletion practices.

If you email me, I receive your email address, sender name if provided, message and any attachments you choose to send. I use that information to handle your enquiry. Providing support information is voluntary, but I may be unable to resolve a request without the details needed to understand it. Google’s Gmail service and the sender’s email provider also process the correspondence and delivery information, such as message times and routing headers.

For a simple enquiry from a child that can be resolved with one reply, I will use the email address only for that reply, not for any other purpose. As soon as possible after replying, I will delete the incoming message, my reply and their attachments from my mailbox and permanently remove the corresponding messages from Gmail Trash. I will not use the address to contact the child again. This limited procedure does not cover ongoing support or requests involving other personal information about a child.

If a child’s email enquiry would require continued correspondence, or includes additional personal information such as a photograph, I will not continue the support exchange directly with the child. Instead, I will ask for a parent or guardian to contact me separately, with the child’s personal details removed from the question. I will promptly delete the child’s original correspondence, any reply I sent and their attachments, including the corresponding messages in Gmail Trash. Further support will address only the question without the child’s personal details.

Ordinary email support correspondence from adults, including parents or guardians, will be kept while the issue is being handled and deleted one week after resolution, including attachments within those messages. Gmail’s ordinary deletion moves those messages to Trash, where they may remain for up to 30 further days before automatic permanent deletion. The faster child-enquiry rule above includes removing the corresponding Trash messages rather than waiting for that period.

Deletion from my mailbox and Trash does not immediately erase every provider-held copy. Google’s server and backup deletion processes can take longer; Google may retain limited information for legal or security purposes under its own policies. Copies held by the sender or other recipients are not removed by deleting my copy.

The support mailbox’s Gmail setting for creating contacts for auto-complete is set to “I’ll add contacts myself.” This setting does not remove previously saved contacts. Deleting email messages does not by itself remove separately saved contacts or downloaded copies.

Send only information needed to explain your question. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, signing keys, identity documents or unnecessary information about a child.

7Retention and deleting local data

Game progress and local settings remain on the device until reset, replaced during normal use or removed through device storage controls. Local storage is not a backup, and progress can be lost when data are cleared or devices change.

After you confirm a reset and it completes successfully, the app clears game progress, learning records and its managed game settings, including the saved language. It resets the advertising-offer preference to off and clears the current session’s advertising age choice. It also clears active game state to prevent an old battle from restoring erased progress. If a confirmed reset is interrupted, a temporary recovery record remains until the reset completes. A storage failure can prevent completion.

The adult-test implementations keep local privacy-history and unfinished-process flags to support cautious eligibility checks and recovery after interruption. The version 17 candidate retains these earlier flags and adds records indicating whether its privacy process is pending or completed, whether older privacy history is uncertain, and whether conflicting regional or choice information has been observed.

Version 17 also stores a SHA-256 digest derived from locally available IAB privacy records, including their field names, types and values and any Additional Consent record present. The digest is used to compare privacy state between checks. It is not identity verification, proof of a person’s consent or a guarantee of anonymity. These additional records use the app’s private Android SharedPreferences storage. The app reads the SDK’s IAB records but does not create a separate persistent copy of their original values.

The app’s own code does not upload these additional safety records to a developer server. Google SDKs separately store and process information as described in section 5. This statement about the app’s additional records is not a claim that the SDKs send no data.

These records have no scheduled deletion period. Some values are updated or replaced during normal checks; older safety flags may remain. A privacy signal becoming too old or unsupported to permit ads does not itself delete the stored record. Game-progress reset, hiding ad offers and rechecking privacy do not erase these records or the SDK’s privacy choices. An unresolved or failed process can leave blocking state in place. Version 17 does not use an old saved digest to grant permission automatically in a new process.

Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling may remove local records, but does not retract requests already sent or guarantee deletion of information already processed by Google. These device controls can also erase game progress. These controls do not themselves change privacy choices held by providers.

The app disables its Android automatic-backup setting. Device-manufacturer tools and other services may create copies outside the app’s control. The reset does not delete those copies.

There is no developer-operated cloud copy from which I can retrieve, restore or remotely erase local game records. Use the app reset or Android’s storage controls for data on your device, noting the different scope described above. Support enquiries are separate and follow section 6. Service providers apply their own retention and deletion practices.

Simulasi tetapan umur untuk orang dewasa sahaja yang diterangkan di bawah masih tersedia tertakluk kepada semakan sedia ada. Calon versi 21: ujian kelajuan berasingan bagi umur dilindungi

Aliran ini dilaksanakan untuk pilihan jelas pemain semasa “Under 13,” “13–17” atau “Prefer not to say.” Ia berasingan daripada simulasi tetapan umur dewasa. Untuk aliran ini, aplikasi menetapkan Google Mobile Ads kepada layanan CHILD untuk kanak-kanak dan penarafan kandungan G sebelum permulaan dan pemuatan, menggunakan tetapan permintaan tidak diperibadikan dan hanya membenarkan unit iklan berhadiah demo Google yang tetap. Semakan ini membaca isyarat privasi setempat yang sedia ada. Ini bukan bukti kelayakan serantau atau persetujuan Google atau ibu bapa. Isyarat TCF, walaupun positif, isyarat US GPP atau USPrivacy, maklumat yang tidak disokong atau rosak, kegagalan membaca dan penolakan dewasa semasa yang diperhatikan menyekat aliran dilindungi ini. Setelah diperhatikan, keadaan yang menyekat itu kekal terkunci bagi instans tersebut. Rekod sejarah atau tanda proses belum selesai yang terdahulu sahaja tidak dianggap sebagai penolakan atau persetujuan.

Kebenaran sekali guna dan keadaan semakannya hanya wujud dalam ingatan; tiada medan privasi kekal baharu yang diuruskan oleh aplikasi ditambah. Perubahan umur, tawaran atau privasi yang berkaitan membatalkan kebenaran yang belum digunakan. Meninggalkan latar hadapan membatalkan kebenaran yang belum digunakan bagi iklan yang belum dipaparkan; iklan SDK yang sudah dipaparkan tidak ditutup secara paksa. Kawalan ini tidak menarik balik permintaan yang sudah dihantar, memadam data yang dipegang oleh penyedia atau menjamin bahawa kerja latar belakang SDK berhenti. Mesej serantau dan had ujian

Mesej yang dikonfigurasi untuk Kawasan Ekonomi Eropah, United Kingdom dan Switzerland menawarkan persetujuan, penolakan dan pengurusan pilihan dalam bahasa Inggeris dan Cina Ringkas. Mesej bahasa Inggeris yang dikonfigurasi untuk negeri AS yang disokong oleh perkhidmatan mesej negeri AS Google menawarkan pilihan untuk membenarkan atau menolak penjualan atau perkongsian data. Ini tidak bermaksud bahawa mesej yang sama terpakai di setiap negeri AS atau bahawa setiap hak serantau diliputi oleh pilihan tersebut. Mesej yang dikonfigurasi atau diterbitkan bukan bukti bahawa aplikasi telah memaparkannya. Skrin yang berkenaan serta maklumat rakan kongsi dan tujuannya menerangkan pilihan yang diminta.

Versi 16 mengikuti peraturan ujian yang lebih berhati-hati: permintaan pengiklanan baharu memerlukan semakan UMP semasa yang berjaya, persetujuan dan pilihan privasi kedua-duanya dilaporkan sebagai “tidak diperlukan,” SDK membenarkan permintaan, serta tiada sejarah privasi yang menyekat, proses yang belum selesai, pilihan privasi yang direkodkan atau isyarat serantau yang tidak disokong. Sesi yang melibatkan pilihan privasi serantau dikecualikan walaupun pemain memberikan persetujuan.

Calon versi 17 pula menyemak dua keadaan yang terhad: subset isyarat privasi Google TCF yang lengkap dan telah disemak, atau petunjuk yang jelas dan disokong bahawa keperluan persetujuan yang berkaitan tidak terpakai. Ia tidak menyimpulkan kebenaran daripada ketiadaan isyarat, penutupan borang atau status SDK sahaja. Semakan TCF menggunakan versi CMP, senarai vendor dan dasar yang tetap serta telah disemak. Format atau versi yang lebih baharu boleh menyebabkan iklan kekal ditutup sehingga disemak. US GPP, USPrivacy lama dan isyarat serantau lain yang tidak disokong masih berada di luar aliran iklan yang disokong oleh calon ini. Permintaan iklan baharu kekal ditutup jika kebenaran yang diperlukan oleh ujian ini tiada, ditolak atau ditarik balik, atau jika isyarat privasi rosak, tidak konsisten atau berada di luar subset yang disokongnya. Ujian ini tidak memerlukan persetujuan kepada setiap penyedia yang disenaraikan, dan lulus semakan tidak membuktikan kebenaran untuk setiap penyedia atau tujuan. Isyarat ketidakpakaian yang muncul kemudian tidak memadam sejarah lama yang tidak pasti atau percanggahan yang telah diperhatikan.

Semakan terhad ini tidak membuktikan bahawa persetujuan tidak diperlukan di bawah setiap undang-undang yang terpakai atau bahawa mana-mana versi menyokong setiap rantau. Kelakuan calon versi 17 dengan respons SDK sebenar, peranti sebenar serta kegagalan storan atau proses Android belum disahkan.

Setiap kali Android memulakan proses aplikasi baharu, semakan baharu dan pengaktifan setempat yang jelas diperlukan. Rekod tersimpan sahaja tidak boleh mengaktifkan iklan. Dalam proses sama yang telah diaktifkan, kembali ke latar hadapan boleh memulihkan ketersediaan selepas semakan baharu berjaya mengesahkan isyarat privasi yang tidak berubah dan rekod setempat yang sah, tanpa meminta persetujuan Google sekali lagi.

Dalam aliran dewasa, jika anda memilih “Advertising privacy choices” secara berasingan, aplikasi masih boleh menghubungi Google untuk memaparkan atau mengurus borang itu walaupun tawaran iklan disembunyikan. Ia tidak menarik balik pilihan yang disimpan oleh UMP, memadam maklumat yang telah diproses oleh Google, membatalkan permintaan yang sudah dihantar atau menjamin bahawa kerja latar belakang oleh SDK yang telah dimulakan berhenti. Perubahan ini tidak membatalkan kerja SDK yang telah dihantar, memadam maklumat yang telah diproses oleh Google atau menutup secara paksa iklan yang sudah dipaparkan. Ujian konfigurasi yang dikawal oleh orang dewasa ini tidak mengesahkan persetujuan ibu bapa, pengiklanan kepada kanak-kanak sebenar, sumber iklan yang dibenarkan atau keperluan keluar dalam lima saat.

6Pertanyaan sokongan dan penyimpanan maklumat

Seksyen ini menerangkan cara saya mengendalikan pertanyaan sokongan di bawah dasar ini.

Saya menerima pertanyaan melalui e-mel, telefon dan SMS. Saya tidak menyediakan sokongan melalui pos. Alamat dalam seksyen 12 diberikan untuk pengenalpastian pengendali, bukan sebagai perkhidmatan sokongan pelanggan melalui pos. Ini tidak mengehadkan hak atau kewajipan di bawah undang-undang yang terpakai.

Jika anda menghubungi saya melalui telefon, saya menerima maklumat yang anda beritahu dan mungkin dapat melihat nombor telefon anda. Jika anda menghantar SMS, saya menerima kandungannya dan maklumat pengirim yang tersedia. Penyedia perkhidmatan telefon dan SMS mungkin memproses komunikasi serta maklumat penghantaran mengikut dasar mereka sendiri.

Untuk sokongan melalui telefon, saya tidak akan membuat rakaman panggilan secara aktif dan hanya akan menyimpan catatan yang diperlukan untuk menyelesaikan pertanyaan. Saya akan memadam mesej SMS sokongan pelanggan dan rekod berkaitan yang saya simpan dalam tempoh satu minggu selepas isu diselesaikan. Jika seorang kanak-kanak memberikan maklumat peribadi melalui telefon atau SMS, saya akan memadam maklumat itu daripada mesej dan rekod berkaitan yang saya simpan secepat mungkin serta meminta ibu bapa atau penjaga menghubungi saya secara berasingan.

Aturan pemadaman ini terpakai kepada rekod sokongan yang berada di bawah kawalan saya. Memadam salinan saya tidak memadam rekod yang disimpan secara berasingan oleh penyedia telefon atau SMS, atau salinan yang dipegang oleh pengirim atau penerima lain. Penyedia menggunakan amalan penyimpanan dan pemadaman mereka sendiri.

Jika anda menghantar e-mel kepada saya, saya menerima alamat e-mel, nama pengirim jika diberikan, mesej dan lampiran yang anda pilih untuk dihantar. Saya menggunakan maklumat itu untuk mengendalikan pertanyaan anda. Pemberian maklumat sokongan adalah sukarela, tetapi saya mungkin tidak dapat menyelesaikan permintaan tanpa butiran yang diperlukan untuk memahaminya. Perkhidmatan Gmail Google dan penyedia e-mel pengirim turut memproses surat-menyurat serta maklumat penghantarannya, seperti masa mesej dan pengepala penghalaan.

Bagi pertanyaan ringkas daripada kanak-kanak yang boleh diselesaikan dengan satu balasan, saya akan menggunakan alamat e-mel itu hanya untuk balasan tersebut, bukan untuk sebarang tujuan lain. Secepat mungkin selepas membalas, saya akan memadam mesej yang diterima, balasan saya dan lampirannya daripada peti mel saya serta memadamkan mesej berkaitan secara kekal daripada Sampah Gmail. Saya tidak akan menggunakan alamat itu untuk menghubungi kanak-kanak tersebut semula. Prosedur terhad ini tidak meliputi sokongan berterusan atau permintaan yang melibatkan maklumat peribadi lain tentang kanak-kanak.

Jika pertanyaan e-mel seorang kanak-kanak memerlukan surat-menyurat berterusan, atau mengandungi maklumat peribadi tambahan seperti gambar, saya tidak akan meneruskan pertukaran e-mel sokongan secara langsung dengan kanak-kanak itu. Sebaliknya, saya akan meminta ibu bapa atau penjaga menghubungi saya secara berasingan, dengan butiran peribadi kanak-kanak itu dibuang daripada soalan. Saya akan segera memadam surat-menyurat asal kanak-kanak itu, sebarang balasan yang saya hantar dan lampirannya, termasuk mesej berkaitan dalam Sampah Gmail. Sokongan seterusnya hanya akan menangani soalan tanpa butiran peribadi kanak-kanak itu.

Surat-menyurat sokongan e-mel biasa daripada orang dewasa, termasuk ibu bapa atau penjaga, akan disimpan semasa isu dikendalikan dan dipadam satu minggu selepas penyelesaian, termasuk lampiran dalam mesej tersebut. Pemadaman biasa Gmail memindahkan mesej itu ke Sampah, dan mesej mungkin kekal di situ sehingga 30 hari lagi sebelum dipadamkan secara kekal secara automatik. Peraturan lebih cepat untuk pertanyaan kanak-kanak di atas termasuk memadam mesej berkaitan daripada Sampah dan tidak menunggu tempoh tersebut.

Pemadaman daripada peti mel dan Sampah saya tidak serta-merta memadam setiap salinan yang dipegang oleh penyedia. Proses pemadaman pelayan dan sandaran Google boleh mengambil masa lebih lama; Google mungkin menyimpan maklumat terhad untuk tujuan undang-undang atau keselamatan di bawah dasarnya sendiri. Salinan yang dipegang oleh pengirim atau penerima lain tidak dipadam apabila saya memadam salinan saya.

Tetapan Gmail bagi peti mel sokongan untuk mencipta kenalan bagi pelengkapan automatik ditetapkan supaya saya menambah kenalan sendiri secara manual. Tetapan ini tidak memadam kenalan yang telah disimpan sebelum ini. Memadam mesej e-mel sahaja tidak memadam kenalan yang disimpan secara berasingan atau salinan yang dimuat turun.

Hantar hanya maklumat yang diperlukan untuk menjelaskan soalan anda. Jangan hantar kata laluan, butiran kad pembayaran, kunci penandatanganan, dokumen pengenalan atau maklumat kanak-kanak yang tidak diperlukan.

7Penyimpanan dan pemadaman data setempat

Kemajuan permainan dan tetapan setempat kekal pada peranti sehingga ditetapkan semula, diganti semasa penggunaan biasa atau dipadam melalui kawalan storan peranti. Storan setempat bukan sandaran, dan kemajuan boleh hilang apabila data dipadam atau peranti ditukar.

Selepas anda mengesahkan penetapan semula dan proses itu selesai dengan jayanya, aplikasi memadam kemajuan permainan, rekod pembelajaran dan tetapan permainan yang diuruskannya, termasuk bahasa tersimpan. Ia menetapkan semula pilihan tawaran iklan kepada dimatikan dan memadam pilihan umur pengiklanan bagi sesi semasa. Ia turut memadam keadaan permainan aktif untuk mengelakkan pertempuran lama memulihkan kemajuan yang telah dipadam. Jika penetapan semula yang disahkan terganggu, rekod pemulihan sementara kekal sehingga proses selesai. Kegagalan storan boleh menghalang proses daripada selesai.

Pelaksanaan ujian dewasa menyimpan penanda setempat bagi sejarah privasi dan proses yang belum selesai untuk menyokong semakan kelayakan yang berhati-hati serta pemulihan selepas gangguan. Calon versi 17 mengekalkan penanda terdahulu ini dan menambah rekod yang menunjukkan sama ada proses privasinya sedang menunggu penyelesaian atau telah selesai, sama ada sejarah privasi lama tidak pasti, dan sama ada maklumat serantau atau pilihan yang bercanggah telah diperhatikan.

Versi 17 juga menyimpan ringkasan SHA-256 yang diperoleh daripada rekod privasi IAB yang tersedia secara setempat, termasuk nama medan, jenis dan nilainya serta mana-mana rekod Additional Consent yang wujud. Ringkasan itu digunakan untuk membandingkan keadaan privasi antara semakan. Ia bukan pengesahan identiti, bukti persetujuan seseorang atau jaminan bahawa data tidak boleh dikaitkan dengan seseorang. Rekod tambahan ini menggunakan storan Android SharedPreferences peribadi aplikasi. Aplikasi membaca rekod IAB SDK tetapi tidak menghasilkan salinan kekal yang berasingan bagi nilai asalnya.

Kod aplikasi sendiri tidak memuat naik rekod keselamatan tambahan ini ke pelayan pembangun. SDK Google menyimpan dan memproses maklumat secara berasingan seperti diterangkan dalam seksyen 5. Pernyataan tentang rekod tambahan aplikasi ini bukan dakwaan bahawa SDK tidak menghantar sebarang data.

Rekod ini tidak mempunyai tempoh pemadaman berjadual. Sesetengah nilai dikemas kini atau diganti semasa semakan biasa; penanda keselamatan lama mungkin kekal. Isyarat privasi yang menjadi terlalu lama atau tidak disokong untuk membenarkan iklan tidak dengan sendirinya memadam rekod tersimpan. Menetapkan semula kemajuan permainan, menyembunyikan tawaran iklan dan menyemak semula privasi tidak memadam rekod ini atau pilihan privasi SDK. Proses yang belum selesai atau gagal boleh menyebabkan keadaan yang menyekat kekal. Versi 17 tidak menggunakan ringkasan lama yang tersimpan untuk memberikan kebenaran secara automatik dalam proses baharu.

Memadam storan aplikasi Android atau menyahpasang aplikasi mungkin membuang rekod setempat, tetapi tidak menarik balik permintaan yang sudah dihantar atau menjamin pemadaman maklumat yang telah diproses oleh Google. Kawalan peranti ini juga boleh memadam kemajuan permainan. Kawalan tersebut tidak dengan sendirinya mengubah pilihan privasi yang disimpan oleh penyedia.

Aplikasi menyahaktifkan tetapan sandaran automatik Androidnya. Alat pengeluar peranti dan perkhidmatan lain mungkin menghasilkan salinan di luar kawalan aplikasi. Penetapan semula tidak memadam salinan tersebut.

Tiada salinan awan kendalian pembangun yang membolehkan saya mendapatkan, memulihkan atau memadam rekod permainan setempat dari jauh. Gunakan penetapan semula aplikasi atau kawalan storan Android untuk data pada peranti anda, dengan mengambil kira perbezaan skop yang diterangkan di atas. Pertanyaan sokongan adalah berasingan dan tertakluk kepada seksyen 6. Penyedia perkhidmatan menggunakan amalan penyimpanan dan pemadaman mereka sendiri.

8Pengehosan halaman privasi dan pautan luar

Apabila dasar privasi ini dilihat melalui GitHub Pages, lawatan tersebut menghantar permintaan kepada GitHub. GitHub merekod dan menyimpan alamat IP pelawat untuk keselamatan, sama ada mereka telah log masuk atau tidak. Laman web ini tidak menyimpan simpanan permainan setempat aplikasi.

Halaman dasar ini tidak mempunyai pengiklanan, kod analitik, borang, fon luar atau sumber jauh yang dimuatkan secara automatik. Pernyataan privasi umum GitHub turut menerangkan maklumat teknikal dan penggunaan laman, seperti butiran peranti, masa permintaan dan halaman yang dilihat, bergantung pada perkhidmatan yang digunakan. Dokumentasi GitHub Pages tidak memberikan tempoh penyimpanan tetap untuk log IP pelawat. Peraturan pemadaman e-mel sokongan tidak mengawal log GitHub.

Membuka pautan privasi luar menghantar permintaan ke laman web yang dipautkan. Maklumat privasi penyedianya terpakai kepada pemprosesannya.

10Pilihan anda dan permintaan privasi

Anda boleh menukar keutamaan setempat, mematikan main balik pertuturan dan menetapkan semula kemajuan permainan setempat dalam aplikasi. Seksyen 5 menerangkan kawalan versi 20 semasa, calon ujian berasingan versi 21 bagi umur dilindungi, dan kawalan terdahulu yang berkaitan yang diwarisi daripada versi 17–19. Pilihan privasi pengiklanan, pengaktifan iklan demo setempat dan penetapan semula permainan mempunyai tujuan yang berbeza. Pengaktifan setempat tidak memberikan persetujuan Google, dan penetapan semula permainan tidak memadam rekod privasi atau menarik balik pilihan yang disimpan oleh penyedia. Membaca dasar ini bukan persetujuan kepada pemprosesan data pengiklanan.

Bergantung pada undang-undang yang terpakai, anda mungkin mempunyai hak berkaitan akses, pembetulan, pemadaman, sekatan, bantahan, kemudahalihan atau penarikan balik persetujuan, serta hak untuk membuat aduan kepada pihak berkuasa perlindungan data yang berkaitan.

Untuk pertanyaan tentang pengendalian maklumat oleh Word Tower, gunakan butiran hubungan e-mel, telefon atau SMS dalam seksyen 12. Jelaskan permintaan dan berikan hanya maklumat yang munasabah diperlukan untuk mengenal pasti surat-menyurat atau rekod berkaitan. Bagi data yang hanya berada pada peranti anda, gunakan kawalan setempat. Anda juga boleh menggunakan saluran permintaan privasi Google atau GitHub untuk maklumat yang diproses di bawah dasar mereka sendiri.

Di Malaysia, maklumat aduan tersedia daripada Jabatan Perlindungan Data Peribadi. Hak dan had masa yang terpakai bergantung pada undang-undang dan jenis permintaan.
